MY
PROJECTS

Straight from the bench — from LoRa trackers that whisper over kilometers to a CNN that eyeballs bottles on a Raspberry Pi. Every one of them started as a breadboard mess and ended as a working system, mostly with a PCB in KiCad or Altium along the way.

THE BENCH BUILDS

01 trackerr.lora

Trackerr

A long-distance whisper box

Two microcontrollers and a radio link that doesn't care about Wi-Fi. GPS position, temperature and signal strength get packed into a LoRa signal that hops between an Arduino Nano 33 and an ESP32 — then lands on a tiny OLED screen.

  • GPS + DHT11 + RSSI beamed over LoRa SX1278
  • Arduino Nano 33 (Tx) → ESP32 (Tx/Rx) relay chain
  • Live readout printed on an OLED
  • Breadboard circuit hardened into a real PCB in KiCad & Altium
ESP32 Arduino Nano BLE 33 LoRa SX1278 Neo-6M GPS KiCad Altium
02 fluid.inspect

Fluid Level Detection

An AI bouncer for the bottling line

A CNN learns what a correctly filled bottle looks like from a custom dataset, then gets squeezed into TensorFlow Lite so it can live at the edge. A Raspberry Pi 3 runs the model in Python and decides, bottle after bottle: pass or reject.

  • CNN trained in TensorFlow on a custom dataset
  • Shrunk to TensorFlow Lite for on-device inference
  • Runs on Raspberry Pi 3 with Python
  • Accept/reject call on every bottle
Raspberry Pi 3 Python TensorFlow Lite Colab
03 parking.watch

Parking Management with STM32

The parking lot that never lies

IR sensors keep an eye on every slot while an STM32F030F4P6 keeps score. The state of the whole lot is always one glance away on a 16x2 LCD — and when the last space disappears, it spells out the words every driver dreads.

  • IR sensors track every slot
  • STM32F030F4P6 brains the whole lot
  • Empty/full status live on a 16x2 LCD
  • "THE PARKING IS FULL" when every slot is taken
STM32F030F4P6 IR Sensor LCD 16x2 STM32Cube IDE
04 door.guard

Smart Door Security

A doorman with an email habit

The ESP32 hosts its own login page — the only key to the door. Enter the right ID and password and it swings open; fumble three times and a buzzer howls while an SMTP email pings the owner. Doors have never tattled so efficiently.

  • Self-hosted web page straight off the ESP32
  • ID + password gate control
  • Three wrong tries → buzzer + email alert via SMTP
ESP32 HTML Arduino IDE Buzzer SMTP Email
05 plant.tend

Plant Care

A green thumb in a box

A NodeMCU8266 plays botanical bodyguard — constantly watching soil moisture and humidity so plants never have to beg for water. When things get thirsty it flips the relay, and the whole story syncs to the Arduino IoT Cloud.

  • Capacitive soil moisture + DHT11 monitoring
  • Relay-driven automatic watering
  • Arduino IoT Cloud dashboard for remote control
NodeMCU 8266 DHT11 Soil Moisture Sensor Arduino IoT Cloud

06 amp.sch

Audio Amplifier

From tiny aux to tiny arena

Stereo in, one big voice out. The AUX signal gets collapsed to mono and pushed through an LM386 so a single speaker can finally get some respect.

  • Stereo → mono mixdown
  • LM386 amplification stage
  • Speaker-ready, noticeably louder output
LM386 Capacitors Resistors Speaker
07 pcb.defect

PCB Defect Detection

The eagle-eyed board inspector

A MATLAB-based image-processing rig that compares a reference board against damaged boards and flags exactly what went wrong — missing holes, spurs, broken traces, or all of the above at once, with damage highlighted right on screen.

  • Catches 4 defect types: missing holes, spurs, damaged traces, and combined faults
  • Reference-image comparison against a folder of damaged PCBs
  • Damage highlighted directly on the output image
  • Custom GUI for drop-in usability
MATLAB Image Processing GUI

08 visitor.count

Visitor Counter

A tiny bouncer with a 7-segment brain

An IR sensor catches every person walking through a doorway and hands the count to a CD4026, which does what it does best — driving a 7-segment display one digit at a time, with a diode standing guard for safety.

  • IR sensor triggers on every entry
  • CD4026 counts and drives the 7-segment display
  • 1N4007 diode for protection and accuracy
CD4026 7-Segment Display IR Sensor 1N4007

10 tank.level

Automatic Water Level Indicator

An LED ladder for your tank

Transistor switching does the maths: as the water climbs, more LEDs light up the ladder. At the top, the buzzer sings and the motor shuts off before a single drop spills over.

  • LEDs light progressively as the level rises
  • Buzzer + motor cut-off at the maximum level
  • Built purely from transistors and resistors

11 street.light

Automatic Street Light Using LDR

Sunrise and sunset, on schedule

An LDR keeps an eye on the sky and flips the switch when the sun clocks out. The street light wakes up at sunset, dozes off at sunrise, and never misses a shift.

  • LDR senses ambient light levels
  • Auto-activates at sunset
  • Auto-deactivates at sunrise
